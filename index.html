1. Core Engineering Pillars (21 Technical Domains)

D01 — Power Generation

Hydroelectric (Francis, Pelton, Kaplan turbines), thermal gas turbine cycles, solar PV parks, synchronous generator excitation and P-Q capability charts.

D02 — High Voltage Transmission

225 kV & 90 kV lines, lattice towers, bundled ACSR conductors, corona effect, line surge impedance loading (SIL), insulator strings.

D03 — High Voltage AIS Substations

Air-Insulated Substations (AIS), single/double busbar schemes, 1.5-breaker bays, disconnectors, SF6 circuit breakers, CT/VT instrument transformers.

D04 — GIS & Pedagogical Pilot

Gas-Insulated Substations (GIS) in metal enclosures, Bay Control Unit (BCU) interlocking logic, earth switch interlocking, partial discharge diagnostics.

D05 — Medium & Low Voltage Distribution

15 kV and 30 kV urban loop feeders, Ring Main Units (RMU), pole-mounted H61 transformers, 400 V three-phase / 230 V single-phase LV networks.

D06 — Power Transformers

Grain-oriented magnetic cores, Dyn11/YNd11 vector groups, ONAN/ONAF cooling, Buchholz relays, On-Load Tap Changers (OLTC), dissolved gas analysis.

D07 — Switchgear & Arc Interruption

SF6 and vacuum circuit breaker physics, arc elongation and de-ionization, transient recovery voltage (TRV), rated making and breaking capacities.

D08 — Protection Relaying & Automation

ANSI 50/51 overcurrent, ANSI 21 distance mho/quad zones, ANSI 87T/87L differential, ANSI 67N directional earth fault, ANSI 50BF breaker failure.

D09 — SCADA & IEC 61850 Substation Bus

MMS client/server, GOOSE peer-to-peer tripping, Sampled Values (SV - IEC 61850-9-2LE), IEEE 1588 PTP precision time synchronization.

D10 — Earthing & Lightning (IEEE 80 / IEC 62305)

Substation copper mesh grounding grid design, touch & step voltage safety thresholds, rolling sphere lightning protection, ZnO surge arresters.

D11 — Power Quality & Harmonics

THD_V & THD_I calculation, flicker (Pst/Plt), voltage sags/swells, unbalance factor, passive tuned LC filters, active harmonic filtering (AHF).

D12 — Power System Studies (IEC 60909)

Symmetrical components (Fortescue method), short-circuit calculations (Ik'', ip, Ib, Ith), Newton-Raphson load flow simulations.

D13 — Industrial Electrical Installations

Main low-voltage switchboards (TGBT Form 4b), motor control centers (MCC), variable speed drives (VFD), automatic transfer switches (ATS), diesel generators.

D14 — Renewables & Battery Storage (BESS)

Utility-scale PV grid connection, fault ride-through (LVRT/FRT), lithium iron phosphate BESS, frequency response, microgrid islanding.

D15 — Substation Auxiliaries & DC Systems

110 V / 220 V DC station battery banks, dual battery chargers, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), FM200 clean-agent fire suppression.

D16 — Asset Testing & Commissioning

Transformer oil DGA (Duval triangle), dielectric breakdown voltage, insulation resistance (Megger / PI / DAR), contact resistance, CT saturation curves.

D17 — HVDC & FACTS

LCC-HVDC thyristor vs VSC-HVDC MMC converters, STATCOM, Static Var Compensators (SVC), long-distance asynchronous power transmission.

D18 — Smart Metering & Grid Digitalization

Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI), DLMS/COSEM communication, PLC and cellular backhaul, anti-fraud algorithms, STS prepaid token systems.

D19 — Civil & Mechanical Interfaces

D20 — Regulation & Cameroon Grid Codes

D21 — Decarbonization & Just Transition

SF6-free eco-efficient switchgear (clean air, C4-FN), hydro-solar hybridization, transmission loss reduction, regional power pool integration (PEAC).

2. Cameroon National Power Grid Observatory (RIS & RIN)

Complete geographical and electrical network model of Cameroon's interconnected power grids:

  • Song Loulou Hydro Plant (384 MW): 8 Francis turbines on the Sanaga river; central frequency-control station for the RIS.
  • Nachtigal Hydro Plant (420 MW): 7 Francis units (60 MW each), Cameroon's newest baseload hydro generation asset.
  • Edéa Hydro Plant (276 MW): Historic facility on the Sanaga river supplying industrial consumers (ALUCAM).
  • Memve’ele Hydro Plant (211 MW): 4 Francis units on the Ntem river, evacuated via 225 kV line to Yaoundé.
  • Lagdo Hydro Plant (72 MW): 4 units on the Benue river, supplying the Northern Interconnected Grid (RIN).
  • Kribi Gas Power Plant (216 MW): Wärtsilä gas engines utilizing Sanaga South offshore natural gas.
  • SONATREL Transmission Corridors: 225 kV backbone interconnecting Song Loulou, Mangombe, Bekoko (Douala), Oyomabang and Nyom II (Yaoundé).

3. Engineering Solvers & Interactive Workbenches

  • Short-Circuit Calculation (IEC 60909): Symmetrical initial short-circuit current (Ik''), peak current (ip), breaking capacity (Ib), thermal equivalent current (Ith).
  • Cable Sizing & Voltage Drop (IEC 60364-5-52): Conductor sizing by current-carrying capacity, installation methods, temperature/grouping derating factors, and max length for Delta_U < 3% or 5%.
  • Transformer Sizing & Losses: Nominal apparent power, active/reactive losses, impedance voltage (Usc%), and optimal efficiency load point.
  • Power Factor Correction: Required reactive power Qc to improve power factor from cos(phi1) to cos(phi2), resonance risk estimation.
  • Substation Single-Line Diagram (SLD): Interactive topology with live breaker status, busbar energization highlighting, and interlock violation prevention.
